## A narrower menu · 菜单更窄了

- **The quota row takes two lines.** `5-hour left` sits above `Claude 96% · Codex 100% · Antigravity 100%`. In 0.1.5 it was one long line, and it set the width of the whole menu. The menu is now about as wide as its subtitle.
- The label is shorter too: `5-hour left` instead of `5-hour quota left`.

If you are on the script, `ccx update` picks this release up on its own. The app download is below.

---

- **额度一行拆成两行。** 上面是 `5 小时剩余`，下面是 `Claude 96% · Codex 100% · Antigravity 100%`。0.1.5 里它是一整行长字，把整个菜单撑宽了；现在菜单宽度跟副标题差不多。
- 标题也更短：`5 小时剩余`，不再是 `5 小时额度剩余`。

已经在用脚本的，`ccx update` 会自己更新到这一版；App 下载见本页附件。
