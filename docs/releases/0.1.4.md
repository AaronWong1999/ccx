## All three quotas in the menu, and a faster hand-off to Antigravity · 三家额度一眼看全，转到 Antigravity 更快

- **Antigravity's quota, read from Antigravity itself.** ccx now reads the 5-hour and weekly limits Antigravity reports for its Gemini models, from Antigravity's own local service while it is open. The last reading is kept while it is closed. `ccx usage` lists all three apps.
- **All three 5-hour windows in one row.** The top of the ⇄ menu shows them side by side, for example `5-hour: Claude 21% · Codex 0% · Antigravity 0%`.
- **The switch suggestion now picks among three apps.** When one app passes 90% or runs out, the notification and the top menu item offer its newest session in whichever app has the most room left, instead of always the same other one. An app that is also close or used up is skipped. If all three are out, ccx says when each comes back.
- **Faster import into Antigravity.** A new Antigravity conversation used to wait for the default model to write its one-line "I have the context" reply. That reply now comes from the light Flash Lite model: about 4 seconds on this Mac. The conversation is unchanged after that, and your next message goes to whatever model you picked in Antigravity. An older Antigravity without the model option falls back to the default model.

If you are on the script, `ccx update` picks this release up on its own. The app download is below.

- **直接从 Antigravity 读取额度。** Antigravity 开着时，ccx 从它的本地服务读取 Gemini 模型的 5 小时和每周额度；关掉后保留最后一次读数。`ccx usage` 会列出三家。
- **三家的 5 小时额度并排显示。** ⇄ 菜单顶部一行看全，例如 `5 小时额度：Claude 21% · Codex 0% · Antigravity 0%`。
- **切换建议在三家里挑。** 哪家用到 90% 或用完时，通知和菜单顶部会建议把那边最新的会话切到剩余额度最多的那一家，而不再固定切到另一家。同样快用完或已用完的会跳过；三家都用完时，告诉你各自几点恢复。
- **导入 Antigravity 更快。** 以前新建 Antigravity 对话要等默认模型回一句"已拿到上下文"；现在这句由轻量的 Flash Lite 模型回复，在本机实测约 4 秒。之后对话不受影响，你发的下一条消息仍由你在 Antigravity 里选的模型回答。旧版 Antigravity 不支持指定模型时，自动退回默认模型。

已经在用脚本的，`ccx update` 会自己更新到这一版；App 下载见本页附件。
